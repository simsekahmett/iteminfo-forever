## AtlasLootClassic (GPL-2.0)

`Data/Recipes.lua` is generated from the profession and recipe tables of
AtlasLootClassic / AtlasLoot Continued (`Data/Profession.lua`,
`Data/Recipe.lua`): which spell creates which item, the skill it requires, its
reagents, and the recipe item that teaches it.

## QuestieDB / Questie

`Data/Sources.lua` and `Data/NpcNames.lua` are generated from QuestieDB: which
NPCs sell or drop an item, and each NPC's name, zone and position.
